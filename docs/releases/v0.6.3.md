# QuantDesk v0.6.3 — Historical SIP Data Archive

**Scope:** read-only multi-session Alpaca SIP bar/trade archive, checksums/provenance, integrity and coverage audit, explicit 1m replay export, and lazily loaded archived executed trades for volume profiles.

- Existing v0.6.2 midnight UI, scrub shortcuts, chart semantics, research journal, market regime and live-observation opt-in are unchanged.
- The downloader uses session-sharded compressed CSV, no Parquet/DuckDB dependency yet.
- Import is *never* automatic; credentials remain in the user's local environment, and no orders are enabled.
- Each successful session is promoted atomically; existing completed days are skipped on rerun after checksum verification.
- Profiles remain **experimental**: raw SIP trade-condition eligibility, late reports/corrections and adjustments are **not** verified by the archive.
- No guarantee of exchange-holiday recognition or 390-bar coverage for early closes.
- Tests use **mock Alpaca responses**, not the user's account or a live connection.

See `README_V063_SIP_ARCHIVE.md` for exact Windows PowerShell commands and limitations.
